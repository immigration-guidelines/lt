# Immigration guidance: Lithuania

Published guidance that the authorities of Lithuania issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- **Guidance:** 41 files in this folder. `index.tsv` lists each file with its title, date and source address.
- The files are unmodified copies of what the source published. Where a source refused automated access, copies come from a public web archive; the index says which.
- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in `NOTES.txt` and `not-obtained.tsv` here, and for all jurisdictions in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md), in the repository that also holds the download scripts. Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

Lithuania publishes no guidance outside its register of legal acts. This repository holds 37 acts as plain text taken from the register's open-data copy: 15 orders of agency heads that set criteria or internal steps for their own officials (marked `guidance` in `index.tsv`, though formally legal acts), and 22 ministerial procedure descriptions that serve as the working manuals for residence, asylum, visas, return and detention (marked `procedural act`). The files are the register's text, not the original documents, and annexes were not fetched. The Migration Department's own site and the register's main sites refuse automated access.

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
