Lithuania - published administrative guidance on immigration case handling
Checked 2026-11-15.

Result: Lithuania publishes no informal case-officer manual, circulars or
"methodological recommendations" for visa, residence or asylum decisions. Everything an
official works from in public is a registered legal act: a "procedure description"
(tvarkos aprašas), rules, criteria or a methodology approved by order of a minister or of
the head of the deciding agency and published in the Register of Legal Acts (TAR).
37 such acts are saved here:
  15 flagged "guidance"       - orders of the agency heads themselves (Migration Department
                                director 7, State Border Guard Service commander 7, Police
                                Commissioner General 1) that set criteria, a methodology or
                                internal steps for their own decision-makers. They are the
                                closest thing to guidance, but formally they are still
                                normative orders in the register, not manuals.
  22 flagged "procedural act" - ministerial orders (Interior; Foreign Affairs; Social
                                Security and Labour jointly) approving the procedure
                                description that is the working manual for a case type
                                (temporary residence permits, long-term residence, asylum,
                                national and Schengen visas, EU citizens, return and
                                expulsion, detention, age assessment, ...).
True non-legislative guidance: 0 files.

Who decides
- Migration Department under the Ministry of the Interior (Migracijos departamentas prie
  VRM): residence permits, national visas, asylum, entry bans, return decisions.
- State Border Guard Service (VSAT): border refusals, visas at the border, return,
  detention, identity and threat checks. Police: public-order threat conclusions.
- Ministry of Foreign Affairs / consulates: Schengen visas.

Sources
- USED: open-data copy of the Register of Legal Acts published by the Office of the
  Seimas, https://get.data.gov.lt/datasets/gov/lrsk/teises_aktai/ (models Dokumentas,
  Suvestine, Priedas; robots.txt allows it, Crawl-delay 5 observed). Queried by adopting
  institution ("Migracijos departament...": 342 acts, of which about 250 are individual
  citizenship decisions of 2006-2011) and by title stems (užsienieč, prieglobs, viz,
  gyventi, migracij, išsiun, fiktyv, be pilietybės, laikinosios apsaugos, Šengeno).
  From about 7,100 hits the valid base acts of the Interior and Foreign ministries, the
  Migration Department, VSAT and the Police that govern how a case is decided were picked
  by hand (list in download_lt.py). Amending orders, forms, fees, registers/IT systems,
  EU-fund and quota acts, and all citizenship acts were left out.
- NOT USABLE: https://www.e-tar.lt/ , https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/ and
  https://migracija.lrv.lt/ all answered the very first request (robots.txt) with a
  Cloudflare "Just a moment..." challenge, HTTP 403; https://www.lrs.lt/ returned a plain
  403. None of them was used after that, nothing was worked around.
- Internet Archive: one CDX query for migracija.lrv.lt files whose names contain
  metodin/rekomendac/gair/atmintin/instrukc/vadov returned only applicant leaflets
  (MIGRIS how-to-apply memos, application-filling instructions) and staff CV pages;
  a second query was refused (connection failed) and the archive was not used again.
- Web searches (Lithuanian) for "metodinės rekomendacijos", "rekomendacijos",
  director's orders on examining residence permit and asylum applications: no
  published recommendations; results were court rulings, Seimas Ombudsman reports,
  EMN studies and the department's annual reports.

About the files
- Each .txt is the register's own text field for the act, written out unchanged as
  UTF-8: the consolidated version currently in force where the register has one
  (normally headed "Suvestinė redakcija nuo ..."; 20 files), otherwise the act as
  adopted (17 files). index.tsv says which, with the open-data URL the text came from
  and the e-tar.lt page of the act. File dates are adoption dates (2004-2026).
- The original DOCX/PDF files and the annexes (mostly forms) are on e-tar.lt and were
  not fetched (bot check). Annex texts exist in the open-data model "Priedas" and could
  be added by dokumento_id if wanted.
- 4-232 (2010) and 4-321 (2019) of the border guard have the same title; both are
  marked valid in the register and both are kept.

Completeness doubts
- The selection is by title keywords; an act with an unusual title could be missed
  (e.g. State Security Department or Employment Service orders that feed into residence
  decisions were not searched for).
- Anything the Migration Department publishes only on its own website (FAQ-style
  practice notes, if any) could not be checked because of the bot check.
- Unregistered internal orders of the Migration Department director (not published in
  TAR) are not public.

Not obtained: see not-obtained.tsv.
